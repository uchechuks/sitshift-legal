Delete your SitShift account and data

SitShift, by AYA HQ · applies to the SitShift app

Deletion is built into the app, takes a few taps, works for guest and email accounts, and is immediate and permanent: your cloud space, your account record, and everything on your phone.

Want a copy first?

Open Profile → Download your data to save one file with everything SitShift keeps about you, before you delete it.

Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open SitShift and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm. That's it.

What gets deleted

  • Your cloud space: your profile and settings (including reminder times), your daily check-ins and completed sessions, your streak, levels and rewards, your health profile if you set one up, your subscription record, and anything from older versions (pod membership, the desk companion's check-in).
  • Your account record: the guest identifier, or your email address and sign-in details.
  • Everything on your phone: your safety answers, starting checks and plan, programs and past programs, workout log, Pregnancy & Postpartum and Joint-friendly settings, wins, weight history, body measurements and progress photos, journal, reminders and all other app data.

Nothing is kept. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. An active subscription isn't cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings so you're not charged again.

If you can't open the app

If you created an account with your email, email developer@ayahq.com from that address and ask for deletion. We'll delete your cloud space and account record and confirm by reply.

If you used SitShift as a guest and uninstalled it, the data on that phone is gone with the app. Anything in the cloud sits under a random identifier with no name or email attached, and there's no way, for you or us, to link it back to you.
