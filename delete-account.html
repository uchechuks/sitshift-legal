Delete your SitShift account & data

SitShift, by AYA HQ · Applies to the SitShift mobile app

Deletion is built into the app, takes a few taps, works for both anonymous and email accounts, and is immediate and permanent — cloud data, account record, and everything on your device. No waiting period, no "are you sure" emails, no retained copies.

Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open SitShift and go to the More tab.
  2. Scroll to the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm. That's it.

What gets deleted

  • Your cloud data: your profile (name, age, preferences, streak, XP, SitCoins, achievements), all daily activity logs (completed moves, including those done with the desk companion, check-in ratings and notes), your presence in any pod (the app leaves the pod first), and the desk companion's note of when it is nudging.
  • Your account record in our authentication system — the anonymous ID, or your email address and credentials if you created an account.
  • Everything on your device: journal entries, body measurements, progress photos, and all local app data.

Nothing is retained. Deletion is effective immediately and cannot be undone. Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, where enabled, are not tied to your account and contain nothing that identifies you (see the Privacy Policy).

If you can no longer access the app

If you created an account with your email address, send a deletion request to developer@ayahq.com from that same email address, and we'll delete your cloud data and account record server-side, then confirm by reply.

If you used SitShift anonymously and uninstalled the app, the anonymous account can no longer be signed into — its data sits under a random identifier with no name, email, or other identity attached, and there is no way (for you or for us) to link it back to you.
