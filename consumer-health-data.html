Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be set on publication

The short version: SitShift handles some health-related information to keep your training safe and fitted to you. Almost all of it stays on your phone, encrypted, and we never sell it, use it for advertising, or share it for anyone else's purposes.

This policy is for people in US states with consumer health data laws (for example Washington, Nevada and Connecticut). It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else.

The consumer health data SitShift handles

  • Safety answers: whether you have certain conditions or symptoms (for example a heart condition, chest pain, high blood pressure, recent surgery, or being told to limit activity), the areas to go easy on, and whether a clinician has cleared you. On your phone only, encrypted.
  • Starting checks and plan: what you can do comfortably and your check results. On your phone only, encrypted.
  • Session reports: how hard a session felt, any discomfort or pain and where, warning signs that stopped a session, and your notes. On your phone only, encrypted.
  • Pregnancy and postpartum: your stage and whether your postnatal check is done. On your phone only, encrypted.
  • Joint-friendly mode and safety flags. On your phone only, encrypted.
  • Body data: weight history, body measurements and progress photos. On your phone only, encrypted.
  • Health profile (optional): sex, height, starting, current and goal weight, activity, experience and health goal. Synced to your private cloud space so your targets follow you to a new phone.
  • What you type in Ask the Coach about an ache, when its AI is on. Sent to produce a routine and not stored by us (see "Who we share it with").
  • Exercise activity: the sessions and moves you complete each day. Synced.

Where it comes from

Only from you, through the app. SitShift doesn't buy data, doesn't infer health information from other sources, and doesn't read health data from your phone in this version.

Why we use it

Only to provide the app you asked for: choosing safe moves, showing a referral instead of a workout when your answers call for one, adjusting your program to how sessions felt, pausing it if you report a warning sign, setting your targets, and showing your progress. Never for advertising, never sold, and never used to decide anything about you outside the app.

Who we share it with

  • No one, for their own purposes. We don't sell consumer health data.
  • Service providers, on our instructions only: Google Firebase stores the synced health profile and activity. If you use Ask the Coach with its AI on, what you type passes through our coach service (hosted by Render) to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the routine. We don't keep it.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Your consent and your rights

You choose what to tell SitShift, and the optional parts (the health profile, Ask the Coach, pregnancy and joint settings) stay off until you use them. You can:

  • see everything: Profile → Download your data;
  • delete it: Profile → Delete account & data, or the account deletion page;
  • withdraw consent for any part by turning the feature off or deleting the answers;
  • ask us which service providers have handled your data, or anything else, at developer@ayahq.com.

We'll respond within 45 days. If we decline, you can appeal by replying; if you're not satisfied, you can contact your state's attorney general.
