SitShift Privacy Policy

Effective July 8, 2026

The short version: your wellness data — moves, check-ins, streaks, coins — lives on your device and is never uploaded. An account is optional, and the only things that may ever leave your phone are anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics that help us fix bugs and improve the app. No ads. No selling or sharing of data. Ever.

What stays on your device

SitShift keeps a local record of your activity so it can work: which micro-exercises you complete, your daily check-ins (energy and sleep ratings, and any note or journal entry you write), your streak, XP, SitCoins, achievements, and the preferences you set (such as your work hours, nudge interval, and an optional first name and age band used to personalize suggestions).

All of this stays in your phone's local storage. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. We run no servers holding your wellness data and have no way to see it.

Accounts are optional

You can use everything in SitShift as a guest. If you choose to create an account, the current version stores it on your device only: your email address is kept locally and your password is stored only as a salted, one-way hash — it is not sent to us. If a future version adds online sync or backup, it will be opt-in, and this policy will be updated first.

What may leave your device — and what never does

To keep SitShift working well, the app may send two kinds of anonymous technical data:

  • Crash reports (processed by Sentry): if the app crashes, we receive the technical error trace along with your device model, operating system version, and app version, tied to a random identifier. This tells us what broke so we can fix it.
  • Usage statistics (processed by PostHog): anonymous counts of feature use — for example that a move was completed, a daily goal was reached, or a notification was tapped — tied to a random install identifier.

Never included in either: your name, email, check-in notes or ratings, journal entries, or anything you type. The random identifiers are not linked to your identity — we couldn't connect them to you even if asked. Both services act as our data processors and are contractually barred from using this data for anything else.

What SitShift does not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties for their own use.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or health records.
  • No uploading of your wellness data — moves, check-ins, notes, journal, streaks — anywhere.

Purchases

If you buy SitShift Premium, the payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details; we only record on your device that Premium is active.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, SitShift schedules movement reminders directly on your device during the work hours you choose. Scheduling happens entirely on your phone; no notification service of ours is involved. You can turn them off anytime in the app or in your system settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data anytime from More → Delete account & data — deletion is permanent. Because your wellness data lives on your device, deleting the app also deletes all of it. Crash reports and usage statistics are anonymous, cannot be traced back to you, and are retained only as long as needed to improve the app. If you'd like the records associated with your random identifier removed anyway, email us and we'll handle it.

Children

SitShift is designed for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of SitShift ever adds an online feature (for example, optional account backup), this policy will be updated first, the change will be clearly announced in the app, and any such feature will be opt-in.
