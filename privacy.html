SitShift Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be set on publication · what changed

The short version: SitShift is a home fitness app, and the most personal things you tell it stay on your phone. Your safety answers, starting checks, programs, workout notes, and any pregnancy or joint settings are kept only on your device, encrypted. Your profile, settings and daily progress sync to a private cloud space so a new phone doesn't mean starting over. You can start as a guest with no email. No ads, no selling or sharing of your data, no tracking across apps. You can download or delete everything in the app, any time.

This policy explains what SitShift collects, why, where it's kept, who else handles it, and your choices. SitShift is provided by [legal name of the business], trading as AYA HQ ("AYA HQ", "we", "us"), [postal address]. This policy covers the SitShift apps for iPhone and Android and the SitShift web version; where the web version works differently, this policy says so. If you also live in a US state with a consumer health data law, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy applies too.

Where SitShift is offered

SitShift is offered only in the United States. It isn't listed in the App Store or Google Play in other countries, and it isn't directed at people in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If you use it from somewhere else, US law and this policy apply, and the protections of your local law may not.

Where your data lives

SitShift keeps different kinds of data in different places on purpose. The app shows the same list under Profile → Your data, where it lives.

Only on your phone, encrypted

On iPhone and Android, sealed with a key held in your phone's secure storage. Never uploaded, not even to your own cloud space. (On the web version, see below.)

  • Safety answers: your answers to the safety questions, the areas you asked us to go easy on, and the date you confirmed a clinician's OK.
  • Your starting point and plan: what you told us you can do comfortably, your equipment and schedule, your check results and the plan they recommended (with the reasons), and the optional gender answer, which only changes the order of workout collections and programs.
  • Your program: which program and phase you're on, how each session felt (including any pain or warning signs you reported), and every change the plan made, with its reason. Programs you left or switched from are kept the same way.
  • Workout log: each session you finished or stopped, its moves, how hard it felt, any discomfort and where, and your note.
  • Pregnancy and postpartum: your stage, whether your postnatal check is done, and when you confirmed the notice.
  • Joint-friendly mode: whether it's on, and whether you answered its suggestion.
  • Your wins, the weekly streak (weeks you marked as a break), and safety flags.
  • Exercise problem reports: what you reported about a move's instructions, safety notes or video, and your note. For now these are your own record on your phone; they'll reach our content reviewers only once we've built a reviewed way to send them, and we'll update this policy first.
  • Weight history, body measurements and progress photos (photos are image files on your phone), and your journal and intentions.
  • Data from older versions, only if you used those features before they were removed: diet data, the body map, and steps and sleep read from your phone. Nothing new is added.

Only on your phone

Small conveniences: your daily walk goal, saved moves and preferred instructor, how sessions felt (which sets the next one's difficulty), personal bests, the guided first week, your home training plan if you have one, reminder backoff (how many workout reminders in a row went unanswered), subscription prompts you answered "Not now", the Ask the Coach daily counter, your agreements (which version of our Terms and this policy you agreed to and when, when you gave your health-data consent, and whether Ask the Coach may use its AI), and, if you created an account, a salted hash of your password for signing in offline (never the password itself). Plus data from features in older versions (care paths, desk plans, the desk setup check), only if you used them.

On the web version

The web version keeps the same things in your browser instead of on a phone, and still never uploads them. But a browser has no secure storage for the key, so on the web version your health and safety answers are not protected by encryption on your device: anyone who can use your browser profile, or software running in it, could read them. If you use the web version on a shared or work computer, don't enter health information, or delete your data when you're done (Profile → Delete account & data). For the full protection described above, use the iPhone or Android app.

Synced to your private cloud space

So your progress survives losing or switching phones, these are stored in a database that only your signed-in account can read or write (enforced on the server, not just in the app):

  • Profile and settings: an optional first name, your age, your goal, your preferences (including workout-reminder times and days, and appearance), and your subscription status if you have one.
  • Daily check-ins and moves: which moves and sessions you completed each day, and the energy and sleep ratings you confirmed.
  • Streak, levels and rewards.
  • Health profile (optional): if you set one up, your sex, height, starting, current and goal weight, activity level, exercise experience, health goal and units, used only to set your own targets. Your weight history isn't here; it stays on your phone.
  • From older versions only: pod membership and the desk companion's last check-in.

The cloud space is hosted for us by Google Firebase on servers in the European Union (Belgium), encrypted in transit and at rest. If you use SitShift from the United States or elsewhere, this data is stored outside your country. SitShift works offline; changes sync when you're back online.

Accounts: guest by default

When you first open SitShift, it creates a guest account: a random identifier with nothing about you attached. You can use every feature this way. If you choose to create an account, your email address and a password are added (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password), so you can sign back in on another phone.

Health and safety information

We ask before we collect it. Before SitShift asks any health question, the app shows a separate consent screen that explains what it will ask, why, and where the answers stay, and links this policy and our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Nothing is collected unless you agree. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your answers or your account.

SitShift asks about your health only to keep training safe and to fit it to you: the safety questions (which can show a referral to a doctor instead of a workout), the starting checks, and your reports after each session (how it felt, any discomfort or warning sign). These answers stay on your phone (encrypted on iPhone and Android) and are used only by the app on your phone: to choose safe moves, adjust your program, pause it and point you to professional help when you report a warning sign, and show your progress. They are never sold, never used for advertising, and never included in crash reports or usage statistics.

SitShift gives general fitness guidance. It isn't medical advice, it doesn't diagnose, and nothing it does is based on your weight, a photo or AI alone.

Ask the Coach

Ask the Coach suggests a short routine for an ache you describe. When its AI is switched on, what you type is sent to SitShift's coach service, which asks Claude, a model made by Anthropic, for a safe routine from our approved moves and sends it back. The first time, the app asks you before anything is sent, and you can choose the offline coach instead, which keeps everything on your phone. We don't store or log what you typed: the coach service keeps only the time and whether the request worked. Anthropic processes the text only to produce the answer, as our service provider, under a contract that limits it to providing the service to us. If you have Pregnancy & Postpartum mode on, or no connection, the answer comes from the app on your phone and nothing is sent. Don't include your name or other identifying details in what you type.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, SitShift schedules workout reminders on your phone, at the times, days and quiet hours you choose. Scheduling happens entirely on your device. Reminder previews never show health information: they say things like "Your next session is ready". Reminders that keep going unanswered come less often, then pause. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to SitShift Premium, payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details. We receive and keep only what's needed to give you access: the plan, when it started, when it renews or ends, and whether it's a trial. How subscriptions work is explained in our Terms.

Crash reports and usage statistics

SitShift doesn't send crash reports or usage statistics today. If we switch them on, they would be limited to:

  • Crash reports (processed by Sentry): the technical error, your device model, system and app version, tied to a random identifier.
  • Usage statistics (processed by PostHog): counts of what's used, for example that a session was completed, that a program was started, or that the subscription screen was shown, tied to a random install identifier.

Never included: your name, email, health or safety answers, how a session felt, any discomfort or warning sign, notes, measurements, photos, or anything you type. We'll update this policy before switching either on.

What SitShift doesn't do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling of your personal data, and no sharing of it for targeted advertising.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your location, contacts or microphone. The camera is used only if you choose to take progress photos, and those stay on your phone.

Who else handles data

These service providers process data only on our instructions, for the purposes above: Google Firebase (accounts and the cloud space, EU), the App Store and Google Play (purchases), Render (hosting the coach service), Anthropic (the coach's AI, for what you type in Ask the Coach), and, only if we switch them on, Sentry and PostHog. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Download your data gives you one file with everything SitShift keeps about you, including the encrypted data, unsealed for you.
  • Correct it: change your answers, settings and profile in the app.
  • Delete it: Profile → Delete account & data erases your cloud space, your account and everything on the phone, immediately. See the account deletion page.
  • Stop reminders in the app or your phone's settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to know, access, correct or delete your personal data, to get a copy, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising (we do neither). To use them, use the app or email us at developer@ayahq.com. We'll respond within 45 days. If we decline a request, we'll say why, and you can appeal by replying to that email; if you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state's attorney general. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

How long we keep data

  • Data in your cloud space: for as long as your account exists, until you delete it.
  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, delete your account, or uninstall the app.
  • Coach requests: not stored by us. Server logs keep only the time and outcome of a request, for our host's standard log period.
  • If you uninstall without deleting your account, your cloud data isn't removed automatically. For a guest account it sits under a random identifier that identifies no one; for an email account, email us from that address and we'll delete it.

Age

SitShift is for people 18 and over. The app doesn't create accounts for anyone younger, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has used SitShift, email us and we'll delete their data.

Security

On iPhone and Android, health and safety answers are encrypted on your phone with a key in its secure storage. On the web version they aren't encrypted on your device (see On the web version). Data in transit is encrypted, and the cloud space is locked so only your account can reach it. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects your data, we'll tell you as the law requires.
