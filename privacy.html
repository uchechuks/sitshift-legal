SitShift Privacy Policy

Effective July 5, 2026

The short version: SitShift stores everything on your device. We don't collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking.

What data SitShift handles

SitShift keeps a local record of your activity inside the app so it can work: which micro-exercises you complete, your daily check-ins (energy and sleep ratings), your streak, XP, SitCoins, achievements, and the preferences you set (such as your work hours, nudge interval, and an optional first name and age band used to personalize suggestions).

All of this stays in your phone's local storage. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. We have no servers holding your data and no way to see it.

What SitShift does not do

  • No account is required, and none is created.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data — we hold nothing to sell.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or health records.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, SitShift schedules movement reminders directly on your device during the work hours you choose. Scheduling happens entirely on your phone; no notification service of ours is involved. You can turn them off anytime in the app or in your system settings.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting the app deletes all of its data. Nothing remains anywhere else.

Children

SitShift is designed for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of SitShift ever adds an online feature (for example, optional account backup), this policy will be updated first, the change will be clearly announced in the app, and any such feature will be opt-in.
