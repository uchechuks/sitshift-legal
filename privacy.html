SitShift Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026 · what changed

The short version: you can use SitShift without ever telling us who you are. Your movement progress — moves completed, check-ins, streaks — and, if you choose to set one up, your health profile (height, weight, sex and goal) sync to a private cloud space under a random anonymous ID (or your email, if you choose to create an account) so a new phone doesn't mean starting over. Your most personal entries — journal, weight history, body measurements, progress photos — never leave your phone, and are encrypted on it. No ads. No selling or sharing of data. You can delete everything, in the app, anytime.

Where your data lives

SitShift handles two kinds of data very differently.

On your phone only — never uploaded. Your journal entries ("Reflect & plan"), your weight history (every weight you record on the Weight progress screen), body measurements and progress photos, your body-tension map, focus-sprint history, desk-audit results, your adaptive movement level, any safety flags you set in your health profile (for example pregnancy, or a condition you manage), the meals you mark as eaten in Fuel and your food preferences (dietary pattern, foods you'd rather skip), and the app's learned sense of when nudges work best for you. None of this is ever sent anywhere. Journal entries, weight history, body measurements, body-map entries, safety flags, meal logs and food preferences are additionally encrypted at rest on your device, so even someone with a copy of your phone's raw storage can't read them.

Synced to your private cloud space. So your progress survives losing or switching phones, the app stores the following in a database only your signed-in account can read or write (enforced on the server, not just in the app):

  • Your profile: an optional first name and age, your preferences (work hours, nudge pace, and your onboarding answers such as goal and focus areas), and your progress — streak, XP, SitCoins, achievements.
  • Your health profile (optional): if you set one up, your height, current weight, goal weight, sex, activity level, exercise experience and health goal. SitShift uses these only to estimate your own targets — movement minutes, a calorie and protein range, hydration and sleep guidance — and they sync with your profile so those targets follow you to a new phone. These are general wellness estimates, never medical advice. You can change or remove the profile at any time.
  • Daily activity logs: which micro-moves you completed each day (with the minutes of any walk you log), your energy and sleep check-in ratings, the optional movement and mood ratings, and any short check-in note you write.
  • Your pod (optional): if you start or join a pod — a small group of two to eight people who agree to keep each other moving — the other members of that pod can see the name you give the pod, the day you joined, and which days you moved (a yes or no per day, kept for about a month), together with the pod's name, its join code and its team streak. Nothing else about you is visible to them: not your profile, your logs, your health data, or anything kept on your phone. Anyone with the six-character code can join. You can leave at any time, which removes your presence; the last member to leave closes the pod. Deleting your account leaves your pod first.

This data is hosted for us by Google Firebase on servers in the European Union (Belgium) and is encrypted in transit and at rest. SitShift works fully offline; changes sync when you're back online.

Anonymous by default — accounts optional

When you first use SitShift, the app creates an anonymous account: a random identifier with no name, email, or anything else about you attached. You can use every feature this way, indefinitely.

If you later choose to create an account, your email address and a password are added to it (handled by Firebase Authentication — your password is never visible to us), and your existing progress carries over. An account exists so you can sign back in after switching phones; it is never required.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To keep SitShift working well, the app may send two kinds of anonymous technical data:

  • Crash reports (processed by Sentry): if the app crashes, we receive the technical error trace along with your device model, operating system version, and app version, tied to a random identifier.
  • Usage statistics (processed by PostHog): anonymous counts of feature use — for example that a move was completed or a notification was tapped — tied to a random install identifier.

Never included in either: your name, email, check-in notes or ratings, health profile details, safety flags, weights, journal entries, measurements, photos, or anything you type. These identifiers are separate from your account and not linked to your identity.

What SitShift does not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling of your data, and no sharing with third parties for their own use — our service providers (below) may only process data on our instructions.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or health records. The camera is used only if you choose to take progress photos — and those photos never leave your device.

Purchases

If you buy SitShift Premium, the payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, SitShift schedules movement reminders directly on your device during the work hours you choose. Scheduling happens entirely on your phone; no notification service of ours is involved. You can turn them off anytime in the app or in your system settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data anytime from More → Delete account & data. This permanently removes your cloud data (profile and daily logs), your account record, and everything stored on the device. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it. Step-by-step instructions are on our account deletion page.

One honest caveat: if you uninstall the app without deleting your account first, the data in your cloud space is not automatically removed. For anonymous accounts it sits under a random ID that identifies no one; for email accounts, contact us from that email and we'll delete it server-side. You can also email us anytime to request a copy or deletion of the data associated with your account.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication and database hosting, EU region), Sentry (crash reporting), and PostHog (anonymous usage statistics). Each acts as our data processor and is contractually barred from using the data for anything else.

Children

SitShift is designed for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

September 10, 2026 (pods): SitShift gained pods, small groups of two to eight people who keep each other moving. Pods are optional. If you start or join one, the other members can see the name you give the pod and which days you moved, as described under "Where your data lives". Nothing about you is visible to anyone outside your pod, no one is ranked, and you can leave at any time.

September 10, 2026: Fuel became a nutrition companion. Two new kinds of data exist, both kept on your phone only and encrypted there: the meals you mark as eaten (a meal name and a meal time, per day — never a calorie diary) and your food preferences (dietary pattern, foods you'd rather skip, whether Nigerian plates should lead, a budget lean). Neither syncs, neither is used for analytics, and both are removed when you delete your account. Nothing about what syncs changed.

September 4, 2026: SitShift gained a Personal Health Engine. If you choose to set up a health profile, your height, weight, sex, activity level, exercise experience and health goal sync with your profile so your targets follow you across phones; your weight history stays on your phone, encrypted. The daily check-in gained optional movement and mood ratings, which sync with your daily logs like the existing energy and sleep ratings. Safety flags you can set in the health profile (pregnancy, a managed condition, symptoms during exercise, a history of disordered eating) stay on your phone only, encrypted, and are used solely to make the app's guidance gentler and to point you to professional help. Nothing here is shared with anyone, and everything SitShift shows from it is a general wellness estimate, not medical advice.

August 22, 2026: as promised in the previous version of this policy, we updated it before launching online features. SitShift now syncs your progress and daily activity to a private cloud space (anonymous by default), instead of keeping everything device-local. Journal, body measurements, and photos remain on-device only, and are now encrypted at rest. Any future change of this kind will be reflected here first, with the effective date above.
